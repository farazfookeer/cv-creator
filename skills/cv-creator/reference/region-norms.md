# Regional CV norms — quick reference

Used by review and create modes to flag mismatches between a CV and its target region.

## At-a-glance matrix

| Region | Length | Photo | DOB | Marital | Nationality | Date format | Refs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| US / Canada | 1 pg (≤10 yrs); 2 pg senior | **No** | No | No | No | Mon YYYY | "On request" |
| UK | 2 pg | No | Optional | No | No | Mon YYYY / DD/MM/YYYY | "On request" |
| Continental EU | 2 pg | Optional, trending out | Optional | No | If cross-border | DD/MM/YYYY | "On request" |
| Germany | 1–2 pg, tabular | **Yes** (traditional) | **Yes** | Optional | If non-EU | DD.MM.YYYY | At end + signature |
| Academic | 4–10+ pg | No | No | No | No | YYYY | **List 3–5** with contact |
| UAE / GCC | 2–3 pg | **Yes** | **Yes** | Common | **Yes** | DD/MM/YYYY | Common to list |
| Australia | 2–4 pg | No | No | No | Citizenship/visa | DD/MM/YYYY | "On request" |
| Japan (rirekisho) | 2 pg, fixed form | **Yes** | **Yes** | **Yes** | **Yes** | YYYY/MM | Listed |

> *Australia and Japan are reference-only in v1; templates not yet shipped.*

## Per-region detail

### US / Canada

- **Length cap:** 1 page is canonical for ≤10 years experience. 2 pages acceptable only at director+ levels or technical-academic crossover (e.g., research scientists in industry).
- **Forbidden in header:** photo, DOB, marital status, nationality, headshot, religion. ATS systems and HR will reject for liability reasons.
- **Address:** city + state is enough; full street address is dated.
- **GPA:** keep if ≥3.5/4.0 and within ~3 years of graduation. After that, drop unless applying to graduate programs or finance.
- **References:** never list. "Available on request" or omit entirely.

### UK / EU

- **Length cap:** 2 pages standard; 1 acceptable for entry-level.
- **Personal Profile / Summary** at top is more common and longer than US.
- **Class of degree (UK):** "First / 2:1 / 2:2" is the convention. Don't translate to GPA.
- **Hobbies / Interests:** traditional in UK, optional and shrinking. Skip if space-tight; include if it's a genuine differentiator.
- **EU Europass format** exists but is rarely required outside EU institutions and looks generic. Use only if specifically requested.

### Germany (Lebenslauf)

- **Tabellarisch (tabular)** is standard. Two-column layout: dates left, content right.
- **Photo top-right** of page 1 — professional headshot, neutral background. Default include unless target is anonymous-recruitment employer.
- **Persönliche Daten** block: full address, DOB, place of birth, nationality (if non-EU), optional marital status.
- **Lückenlosigkeit:** every gap >2 months must be accounted for ("Elternzeit," "Sabbatical," "Berufliche Neuorientierung"). Silent gaps are red flags.
- **German grade scale:** 1.0 (best) to 5.0 (fail), with decimal commas (1,7 not 1.7).
- **Signature:** date + handwritten signature at the very bottom. Insert as image; keep traditional.
- **Anschreiben:** cover letter is a separate, expected document — out of scope for this skill.

### Academic

- **Length expands with seniority.** Expect 4–10 pages for tenure-track, 15–20+ for full professor with long publication record.
- **Publications dominate.** Subdivide: peer-reviewed journal articles, conference proceedings, book chapters, preprints/under review.
- **Bold/underline your own name** in author lists (norms vary by field — math/theoretical physics use alphabetical authorship and don't bold).
- **Grants funded** — title, agency, role (PI/Co-PI/CoI), amount, dates.
- **References:** academic CVs DO list 3–5 referees with full contact info, unlike industry.
- **Industry-style "Skills" sections** read amateurish — omit or rename to "Languages" / "Methodologies" if needed.

### UAE / GCC

- **Photo expected**, especially for healthcare, hospitality, retail-luxury, customer-facing, and government-adjacent. International tech firms in DIFC/ADGM more relaxed.
- **Visa status** is load-bearing — Employment, Golden, Spouse, Tourist all imply different sponsorship costs.
- **Nationality is screened.** Declining to state is unusual.
- **Healthcare licensure:** DOH (Abu Dhabi), DHA (Dubai), MOH (federal) — non-negotiable for clinical roles. Foreign-trained candidates: surface dataflow/Prometric/license status.
- **Arabic + English proficiency** at the top, even for non-Arabic speakers.
- **Tenure valued.** Stints under 18 months are flagged; explain or contextualize.

## When the user is ambiguous about region

Default heuristics:
- Target country named → use that region.
- Multiple countries (e.g., "applying in Europe broadly") → ask. Don't compose a German Lebenslauf for someone targeting an Amsterdam tech firm.
- Citizenship vs. application destination → **destination wins**. A Canadian applying to a UK firm gets a UK-style CV.
- Cross-border applications (e.g., UAE-based applying to UK roles) → produce two versions if the user wants both markets.
