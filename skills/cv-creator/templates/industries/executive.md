# Executive overlay (Director / VP / C-suite)

Apply on top of any region template. Length expectations expand: 2 pages strict floor for senior executives; 3 acceptable for board-level.

## Section emphasis

- **Leadership Profile** (4–6 lines, replacing summary) — sector, scale of organizations led, signature outcomes, leadership philosophy in one phrase.
- **Career Highlights / Selected Achievements** — promoted ahead of Experience. 3–5 single-line headline accomplishments with $ / % / scope figures.
- **Experience** — focus on org-level outcomes (P&L scope, headcount, transformation), not tactical bullets.
- **Board & Advisory** — if applicable, dedicated section.
- **Education** — degrees + executive programs (Harvard AMP, Stanford SEP, INSEAD, IMD).
- **Speaking / Publications / Patents** if relevant.

## Section order (default for senior exec CVs)

1. **Header**
2. **Leadership Profile**
3. **Career Highlights** (promoted)
4. **Professional Experience** — org-level bullets, scope-led
5. **Board & Advisory Roles**
6. **Education & Executive Education**
7. **Speaking / Publications** *(if applicable)*
8. **Awards & Recognition**

## Career Highlights template

```markdown
## Career Highlights

- Scaled {{Function/Business}} from **${{X}}M to ${{Y}}M ARR** ({{Z}}% CAGR) and **{{N}} → {{M}} headcount** over {{Y}} years at {{Company}}.
- Led {{transaction type — IPO / acquisition / divestiture}} of {{$ value}}; resulting {{outcome}}.
- Drove {{transformation}} that reduced {{cost / cycle / churn}} by {{N}}% across {{X}} business units.
- Built and led the {{function}} organization through {{economic event / market shift}}; retained {{N}}% of senior leadership through transition.
- Recruited and developed {{N}} VPs / Directors, {{M}} of whom were promoted to GM/C-level roles within {{X}} years.
```

## Leadership Profile template

```markdown
## Leadership Profile

{{Senior {{function}} executive with {{N}} years building and scaling {{type of organization — high-growth SaaS / global manufacturing / regulated financial services}} across {{geographies}}.}} {{Track record of {{1–2 signature accomplishments — e.g., taking businesses from $X to $Y in revenue, executing $X B in M&A, leading post-merger integrations}}.}} {{Known for {{leadership style descriptor — e.g., operator-first leadership, customer-obsessed transformation, data-led commercial discipline}}.}} {{Sector focus / next-role intent.}}
```

## Bullet patterns

❌ "Managed a large team."
✅ "Led 240-person GTM organization (Sales, CS, Marketing, RevOps) across 6 regions; grew bookings from $42M to $118M (2021–2024) while maintaining net dollar retention >130%."

❌ "Drove digital transformation."
✅ "Sponsored core-banking platform replacement ($18M, 26 months); cut transaction processing latency 60%, retired 14 legacy systems, and freed $4.2M/yr in license costs to reinvest in product."

❌ "Worked on M&A."
✅ "Co-led acquisition and integration of [redacted competitor] ($340M EV); achieved 92% revenue retention through year 1 and exceeded synergy plan by $11M (run-rate)."

## Executive-specific gotchas

- **Bullets are about scope and outcome, not activity.** A VP CV that reads "Created presentations and led meetings" is a junior CV in disguise.
- **Quantify scope.** P&L size, headcount, geographies, products are non-negotiable. Hiring committees and recruiters use these to bracket roles.
- **Confidentiality:** anonymize sensitive M&A or financial figures only when contractually required; otherwise disclose. Vague exec CVs read as unimpressive.
- **2 pages minimum.** Cramming a 25-year career into one page reads as junior or insecure. Use the room.
- **Drop early-career detail.** Years 1–8 of a 25-year career compress to a few lines or a "Earlier roles" footer.
- **Board roles** have their own conventions — list as "Board Director" or "Advisor," include dates, list governance committees if applicable, and note public/private/non-profit.
- **Executive search firms scan for signal**: industry, scale, transformation type, exit/IPO experience, P&L responsibility. Make these unmissable in the first half-page.
