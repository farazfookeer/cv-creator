# Nursing / healthcare clinical overlay

Apply on top of any region template. **Licensure is the single highest-leverage section** — promote it accordingly.

## Section emphasis (override default order)

1. **Licenses & Certifications** — moved up, immediately after Profile/Summary. Without active licensure for the destination jurisdiction, the application is dead.
2. **Clinical Experience** — relabel "Professional Experience" to make scanning easier.
3. **Specialty Skills** — list clinical competencies (procedures, equipment, EHR systems).
4. **Education** — degree (BSN, ADN, MSN), institution, graduation year. Add **NCLEX pass year** for US-trained.
5. **Continuing Education** — recent CE hours, especially specialty-specific.
6. **Professional Memberships** — ANA, RCN, specialty associations.

## Licenses & Certifications block (mandatory)

Every nursing CV must list:

**Primary licensure** (relevant to destination):
- US: RN License — State Board, License #, Issue/Expiry
- UK: NMC PIN, registered effective date
- UAE: **DOH Abu Dhabi / DHA Dubai / MOH UAE** — license #, valid through
- Germany: Anerkennung as *Gesundheits- und Krankenpfleger/in*; Approbation status
- Saudi Arabia: SCFHS classification + Prometric pass year

**Required clinical certifications:**
- **BLS** (Basic Life Support) — provider, exp date — universal baseline
- **ACLS** (Advanced Cardiac Life Support) — for adult acute/critical care
- **PALS** (Pediatric Advanced Life Support) — for peds/ER
- **NRP** (Neonatal Resuscitation) — for L&D, NICU
- **TNCC** / **ENPC** — for ER nurses
- **CCRN / CEN / OCN / RNC** — specialty board certifications

## Region-specific licensure quick reference

| Region | Primary body | Common bridge for foreign-trained |
|---|---|---|
| US | State Board of Nursing | NCLEX-RN; CGFNS for foreign-educated |
| UK | NMC | OSCE + CBT; English: IELTS 7.0 / OET B |
| UAE | DOH (AD), DHA (Dubai), MOH (federal) | Prometric exam, dataflow verification |
| Saudi Arabia | SCFHS | Prometric, classification by experience |
| Germany | Bundesland-specific (Approbation) | Anerkennung process; B2 German |
| Canada | NNAS + provincial regulator | NCLEX-RN, IEN assessment |
| Australia | AHPRA / NMBA | OBA pathway, English (IELTS 7.0) |

## Nursing-specific gotchas

- **Expired credentials = dead application.** Always include expiry date. If anything is within 6 months of expiry, renew before applying — and update the CV.
- **Patient ratios matter** to nurse hiring managers. "Worked in ICU" is meaningless; "1:2 ICU with mixed cardiac/respiratory" is meaningful.
- **EHR experience is screened.** Always name the system(s).
- **Avoid medical jargon overload** if applying internationally — recruiters in HR may not know "CRRT" or "ECMO" by acronym; spell on first use.
- **Foreign-trained nurses applying to UAE/Saudi:** Foreground the dataflow/Prometric/license status. Recruiters need to know whether you're a 2-week hire or a 6-month hire.
- **GCC-region nurses:** A photo, visa status, and Arabic level are frequently expected in addition to clinical content.
