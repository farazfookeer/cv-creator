# Sales / customer-facing overlay

Apply on top of any region template.

## Section emphasis

- **Numbers, numbers, numbers.** Sales CVs live or die by quota attainment %. Every role needs:
  - Quota size
  - Attainment % (and consecutive quarters/years achieved)
  - Average deal size
  - Sales cycle length
  - Pipeline/territory size
- **Promotion velocity** matters. Show progression (BDR → AE → Sr AE → AM → Director).
- **Stack rank** if you've ever been #1 / President's Club / top 10% — *say so explicitly*.
- **Awards** section is appropriate (President's Club, Pinnacle, etc.).

## Must-haves by role type

| Role | Foreground |
|---|---|
| BDR / SDR | Meetings booked/month, conversion rate, # of accounts touched |
| AE (SMB / Mid-Market) | Quota, attainment, ACV, # of new logos closed |
| Enterprise AE | Average deal size (often $250K–$1M+), longest cycle closed, sample logos won |
| Account Manager | Net retention %, gross retention %, expansion ARR |
| Sales Engineer | Win rate on technical evaluations, products supported |
| Customer Success | NRR, GRR, NPS, # accounts owned, $ ARR managed |
| Sales leadership | Team size, team attainment, hiring/ramp metrics, pipeline coverage |

## Bullet patterns

❌ "Sold software to clients."
✅ "117% of $1.4M ACV quota in FY24 (5 of 8 quarters at >100% across last 2 years); closed largest new-logo deal in territory at $620K ACV."

❌ "Generated leads."
✅ "Booked 38 qualified meetings/quarter against a 25 target; 22% converted to AE-stage opportunities, contributing $2.1M to top of funnel."

❌ "Worked with key accounts."
✅ "Managed $4.8M ARR portfolio of 12 enterprise accounts; drove 128% NRR through expansion of three accounts into new business units, retained all renewals (100% logo retention)."

## Sales-specific gotchas

- **Unquantified bullets are dead weight.** A sales CV without numbers signals you didn't hit number.
- **Attainment without quota size is suspicious.** "150% of quota" on a $200K quota is different from a $2M quota — list both.
- **Down years happen.** A 2022 attainment dip during a tech-sector downturn is forgivable; pretending it didn't exist is worse than acknowledging context ("FY23: 89% attainment in macro-driven contraction; rebounded to 117% FY24").
- **Logos sell.** If your customers are recognizable brands and not under NDA, list them (anonymize industry+size if confidential).
- **For sales-leadership CVs:** the metric shifts from your number to your team's — quota coverage, ramp time, attrition, hiring success.
- **Customer success is sales-adjacent, not sales.** NRR / GRR / NPS / TTV (time-to-value) are the right metrics; raw quota attainment usually isn't.
