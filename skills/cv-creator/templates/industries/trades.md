# Trades / blue-collar / skilled labor overlay

Apply on top of any region template. Covers electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs, welders, mechanics, machinists, construction, oil-and-gas field roles, equipment operators.

## Section emphasis

- **Licenses & Certifications** are gating — promote to top section (like nursing). Without the right ticket, the application is dead.
- **Equipment & Tools** — list the specific systems you've worked on (controllers, OEM brands, machinery types).
- **Safety record / training** — flag-worthy: OSHA, NEBOSH, IOSH, SCAFFOLD, IPAF, MEWP, working-at-height, confined-space.
- **Years of hands-on experience** in each specialty (specific, not "experienced").
- **Drive license + endorsements** matter (Class A CDL, HR/HC for AU, Class 1 in UK).

## Section order (override)

1. **Header**
2. **Profile** — 2–3 lines: trade, total years, specializations
3. **Licenses, Certifications & Tickets** *(promoted to top)*
4. **Work Experience** — site/project, role, scope, dates
5. **Equipment & Systems**
6. **Safety Training**
7. **Education / Apprenticeship**
8. **References** — common in trades; site supervisors/foremen are routine references

Region quick reference:
- **US:** State journeyman / master licenses; OSHA 10/30; trade-specific (NICET for fire alarm, EPA 608 for HVAC refrigerants)
- **UK:** CSCS card (color = level), JIB grade (electrical), Gas Safe (plumbing/gas), 18th Edition (electrical), IPAF, PASMA
- **Canada:** Provincial Red Seal, Hot Work, Fall Protection, WHMIS
- **Australia:** White Card, EWP/HRWL, electrical license (state), Confined Space Entry
- **UAE/GCC:** ADNOC/site-specific HSE passports, third-party-verified trade certs (City & Guilds), driver's license

## Trades-specific gotchas

- **List actual hands-on hours / years on specific equipment.** "Crane operator" is vague; "1,800 hrs on 35–80T crawler/hydraulic cranes" is clear.
- **Safety record is a hire/no-hire factor.** Mention LTI-free streak, near-miss participation, toolbox talk leadership.
- **Site reality:** project type, contract value, and crew size on each role help recruiters calibrate.
- **No fluffy summaries.** Tradespeople hiring managers and foremen are skeptical of marketing language. Plain, direct, factual.
- **References are normal in trades.** Listing a former foreman with phone number is expected, not unusual.
- **For oil-and-gas / offshore / GCC site work:** add **medicals** (BOSIET, HUET, OGUK Medical, helicopter underwater escape) and **rig experience** with day counts.
