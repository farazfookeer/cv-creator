# UAE / GCC CV skeleton

**Length:** 2–3 pages standard. 1 page acceptable for entry-level only; 4+ only for executive.
**Photo:** Yes, expected. Professional headshot, top-right. Omitting one is unusual and can disadvantage you, particularly in healthcare, hospitality, and traditional sectors. International tech firms in DIFC/ADGM are more relaxed.
**Personal details:** Name, location (Emirate, UAE), phone (+971), email, LinkedIn. Plus:
- **Nationality** — required (visa/sponsorship implications)
- **Visa status** — Employment / Spouse / Golden / Tourist; helps employer understand sponsorship cost
- **Marital status** — common, especially for relocating candidates
- **Date of birth** — common; some employers screen by age
- **Languages** — Arabic + English proficiency must be near the top
- **Driver's license** — UAE license + manual/automatic, common to list

**Date format:** MM/YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY.
**Bullets:** 3–6 per role; quantify, but match the bullet weight to seniority. Mid-level roles in the GCC often emphasize stability and tenure as much as impact.

## Section order (default)

1. **Header + Personal Details** (with photo top-right)
2. **Profile / Career Summary** — 4–6 lines; longer than US, common in GCC
3. **Languages** (high; often before experience for non-anglophone hiring)
4. **Professional Experience**
5. **Education**
6. **Certifications & Licenses** — heavy emphasis (essential for healthcare → DOH/MOH/DHA licensure; engineering → Society of Engineers UAE)
7. **Skills**
8. **References** — sometimes listed (with contact details), unlike US/UK

## Region-specific gotchas

- **Photo expected**, especially in healthcare, hospitality, retail-luxury, government-adjacent. International tech firms more relaxed.
- **Visa status is load-bearing.** Employment visa transfer vs. fresh sponsorship vs. spouse visa each affect time-to-hire and cost. Employers screen on this.
- **Nationality screening** is a reality (positively or negatively for various nationalities depending on industry); declining to state is unusual and can hurt.
- **Healthcare licensure is non-negotiable.** Nurses/doctors need DOH (Abu Dhabi), DHA (Dubai), or MOH (federal) licenses. Without one, the application is dead. Always foreground these — see `templates/industries/nursing.md`.
- **Arabic + English** language proficiency is a screening dimension. List both even if you're a non-Arabic speaker (it's expected to declare).
- **Tenure is valued** — short stints (under 18 months) are flagged; bullet your reason if it's not obvious.
- **References:** Listing with contact details is acceptable here in a way it isn't in the US/UK. Get permission first.
- **Dress for the destination:** A Dubai DIFC investment-banking CV reads more like a London CV than a Dubai retail CV. Adjust formality based on industry sector within the UAE.
