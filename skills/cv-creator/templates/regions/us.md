# US / Canada résumé skeleton

**Length:** 1 page (strict for ≤10 yrs experience; 2 acceptable only for senior/executive).
**Photo:** No. Including one is grounds for rejection at ATS-using employers (bias control).
**Personal details:** Name, city/state, phone, email, LinkedIn URL only. No DOB, marital status, photo, nationality, headshot.
**Date format:** Month YYYY (e.g., "Mar 2023 – Present").
**Bullets:** 3–6 per role, lead with action verb + quantified outcome.

## Section order (default)

1. **Header** — Name, location (City, ST), phone, email, LinkedIn, optional portfolio/GitHub
2. **Summary** (optional, 2–3 lines) — only useful if you're pivoting or senior; entry-level usually skips
3. **Experience** — reverse-chronological; company, title, location, dates, bullets
4. **Education** — degree, institution, year (drop GPA after ~3 years out of school unless ≥3.7)
5. **Skills** — technical skills, tools, languages (industry-dependent emphasis from overlay)
6. **Projects** *(if relevant — common for entry-level tech)*
7. **Certifications** *(if industry-relevant — overlay decides)*

## Region-specific gotchas

- **No photo.** Ever. Even on the LinkedIn-style "personal brand" CVs.
- **No DOB, marital status, nationality.** US anti-discrimination practice.
- **One page.** Tighten margins (0.5"), drop GPA, drop high school, cut filler before going to 2.
- **Spell out dates.** "3/23" is ambiguous (US vs ISO); "Mar 2023" is not.
- **ATS-safe.** No tables, no text boxes, no headers/footers (parsers drop them), no two-column layouts unless you're in a creative field with a designed PDF.
